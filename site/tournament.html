✂ Eliminations
§ How a tournament worksReference
  1. One ledger, started even.

    Every entrant begins on the same paper stack — 10 ◎ by default — on a ledger that exists only inside the tournament. Your all-time record is never read and never touched: a bad bracket can't hurt it, and a lifetime record can't be donated to a bracket.

  2. The clock is the server's.

    Rounds are fixed windows folded from the tournament's start time — 24 hours by default. When a boundary passes, the cron settles it once, freezes the standings it decided from (hash and all), and cuts the bottom of the board. A boundary can never be re-run or talked into a different result.

  3. The bottom five fall, every round.

    Ranking is tournament P&L — pushed equity minus the start stack, computed server-side. Never pushed a snapshot? You stand at your stack: silence is a number, not a hiding place. When the survivors number no more than the cut, that round is the final — it crowns instead of cutting.

  4. Spectating is data, not video.

    Click any trader on the board and their live card polls every few seconds: equity, cash, positions, last push. It's the extension's own report, labeled as such — the honest version of "watch them trade" that a paper ledger can actually serve.
