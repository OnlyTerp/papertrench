The overlay · demo data

Trade the real chart. Keep a record that can't be quietly edited.

A quick-buy ticket on the terminal you already use, filled at a price that had to come from somewhere real. Then every round graded on what you did, not on whether it worked.

  • Armed buysClick buy before a brand-new coin has a quote. The order arms, then fills the instant the first trusted price lands.
  • Native chart fillsOn Padre, paper buys and sells render as real TradingView markers, with average fill and exit lines.
  • Exit qualityEvery round graded excellent, sold early or round-tripped, with the SOL you left on the table quantified.
  • Session replayScrub any trade: every fill, chart frames, and the screen recording if you made one, on a single timeline.
  • Hash-chained recordEvery fill commits to a hash chain the moment it happens. Ordering, pre-commitment and price are all checkable later.
  • X-RayLand on any X profile and the intel card is already there: account age, name changes, every contract address it has posted.
