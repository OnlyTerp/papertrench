Verifiable leaderboard

A leaderboard that can't be faked

Every fill is committed to a SHA-256 hash chain the moment it happens — before the outcome is known. No inserting trades, no deleting losers, no backdating winners. The dashboard re-derives your record from the chain and standings are recomputed server-side from the raw fills, never from a self-reported number.

⛓️

Tamper-evident by construction

Each fill commits to the hash of the previous one. Touch anything, and every link after it breaks.

🔍

Every price re-verifiable

Fills record mint, price, and timestamp — so a verifier can re-check them against real market history.

🪪

Verified identity

One ranked record per verified account, making sybil entries expensive.

It never invents a price
