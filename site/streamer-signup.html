Streamer application

Get featured in the trench.

Any creator running the PaperTrench Challenge can apply — Twitch, Kick or YouTube. Approved streamers get a card on the streams page, the Streamer role in Discord, and an automatic alert when they go live.

Sending this does not put anything on the site. Your application goes into a private queue that only verified PaperTrench moderators can open. A human reads it, and only if they approve does a card appear on the streams page. If they don't, nothing is published at all.

How we reach you

Moderators only

Moderators need a way to reach you about your application. These answers live in a separate set of columns from the card ones, and the query that builds the public roster does not read them — so there is no path by which they end up on the site.

What is stored. The answers on this page, plus a one-way hash of your IP address for abuse triage — never the address itself. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for marketing. Ask any moderator to delete your application and it is removed, approved or not. Details in the privacy policy.

Application received

It is sitting in the moderator queue now — not on the site. A moderator will read it and reach out on Discord. If they approve you, your card goes up on the streams page automatically.
