    §How a duel settles

    Four rules. The third one is the entire design — everything else follows from it.

    1. A mode is a window, not a book.

      A duel is your existing committed chain sliced to one shared window — the same slice the Sprint uses. There is no separate duel record to inflate, which is why the modes are uncheatable as a set rather than one at a time.

    2. A round counts only if it opened and closed inside the window.

      A bag carried in was decided before the window existed; a bag carried out has no honest realized number yet. Returns are measured against equity at the window's open, so bankroll size is not an advantage.

    3. Only a post-close chain can settle it.

      The chain is extend-only, so a losing round cannot be deleted — the hashes break, and a swapped-in history is refused as chain-replaced. That leaves one vector: submit while you are up, then go quiet. Settling only from chains submitted after the close shuts it, because such a chain necessarily carries every fill made inside the window.

    4. Silence is a forfeit, not a freeze.

      A player who never submits after the close forfeits. Live standings during the window are shown because they are the fun — and labeled provisional because they decide nothing.
