v3.0.0 Feature Aug 6, 2026

Perps arrive — with the honest list of what they don't model

Leveraged paper trading on Hyperliquid and Jupiter, priced with the venue's own fees, funding and liquidation math — four numbers before every entry, and a ticket that refuses to open when the live funding rate can't be read. A liquidation can take your whole position, which is a different lesson from spot, so the notes lead with the seven things perps do not model yet. Plus market-cap alerts, chart take-profit/stop orders, Forge, and Turbo II.

v2.8.1 Fix Integrity Aug 5, 2026

We shipped a build that broke the chain, and said so

v2.8.0 carried half an in-flight migration, and every paper fill on it failed to append to the attestation chain — the tamper-evidence layer the leaderboard verifies against. Wallets, balances and P&L were never touched, the failure showed a toast on every fill, and the gap is disclosed rather than back-filled: a chain entry written after the outcome is known proves nothing. Here is what broke, who it affects, and how the chain was rebuilt.
