Daily Spark

One real chart, frozen at a moment. Call what happens next. Graded on your reasoning, never on profit.

Spark streak and your accuracy, kept in the dashboard

A single mint's chart is cut at a point in time. You say where it goes and why — the why is the part that is graded. Get the direction right for the wrong reason and it does not count for much; the puzzle is about reading a chart, not about being lucky once.

It changes at 00:00 UTC and everyone gets the same chart that day, so nobody can farm it by refreshing. Miss a day and the streak resets — that is the whole pressure, and it is deliberately the only one.

The Weekly Sprint

A fresh board every Monday. Only the fills you take inside the window count, so a good week is not buried under an old one.

Top of the Sprint board for the week, with your verified record
EntryFree
Entrants—
Closes in—

The Sprint is one ISO week, Monday to Monday. Your slice is computed by the same verifier that folds the main leaderboard — the fills are re-hashed, the book is replayed, and every price is checked against real market history before it ranks. Nothing here is self-reported.

Because the window resets, the Sprint is the board a newcomer can actually win. The lifetime leaderboard rewards a long history; this one only asks about this week.

Found a clan or join one with an invite code. A clan counts only what its members traded since they joined, so recruiting somebody with a big history does not import their past — a clan's score is a clan's own work.

A new clan reads forming until it has five counting members, and shows no score at all rather than a zero. An empty clan is not last place; it has not started.

The Friday Reckoning

Every Friday evening the week gets read out: how each clan traded, who held their discipline, what the board did.

Your clan's week, posted to the Discord, by name
EntryBe in a clan
BellFri 20:00 UTC
Rings in—

One anchored ritual a week instead of a stream of notifications. The digest is built from standing data — the same facts the public clan page shows — and it is process only: rounds, score, placement. No profit-and-loss goes in it, by design.

If the bell is missed because something was down, the window stays open until Saturday evening and then the week is left unposted. A late digest pretending to be on time would be worse than a gap.
