§ How the sprint works

Four rules. The chain enforces all of them, which is why there are only four.

  1. One clock, and it runs on UTC.

    The window is UTC Monday 00:00 → the following Monday 00:00 — the same seven days for everyone, with no timezone worth gaming. Which side of the boundary a fill lands on is not a claim you get to make afterwards: the timestamp was hash-committed at the moment the fill happened.

  2. In and out, or it doesn't count.

    A round counts only if it opened and closed inside the window. A bag you carried in was last week's decision; a bag you carry out has no honest realized number yet. A round that straddles the Monday boundary belongs to neither week's sprint — it still counts on the all-time board, which has no window to fall out of.

  3. Same formula, one week's slice.

    Window ROI × ln(1+rounds) × discipline, exactly as on the main board. Nothing about the score is special-cased for the sprint; the only thing that changes is which part of the chain the question is asked about.

  4. No separate entry, and no reset.

    There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to wipe. Your normal practice is your entry — the sprint is a lens on the same committed chain that backs your all-time record, which is precisely why it cannot be gamed separately. Deleting a bad week to start fresh breaks the extend-only anchor and is refused by name.
